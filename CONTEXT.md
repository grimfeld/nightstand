# Readlist

A personal tracker for the books I intend to read, own, have read, and have studied.

## Language

**Acquisition**:
Whether and how I have access to a copy of a book: Wanted, Owned, Borrowed, or Gone. Independent of whether I have read it.
_Avoid_: Purchase, ownership status, bought, unbought

**Gone**:
A book that has left my life — returned, sold, given away, or decided against. Why it went is not recorded; Engagement and the dates already distinguish "read it and released it" from "changed my mind".
_Avoid_: Deleted, archived, rejected

**Engagement**:
How far I have got through a book: Unread, Reading, Read, or Abandoned. Independent of whether I own it.
_Avoid_: Status, progress, reading status

**Nightstand**:
The single ordered set of books currently claiming my attention, hard-capped at a small number of Slots. Holds both books I am reading and books queued behind them. Membership is independent of Acquisition — a Wanted book on the Nightstand is the instruction to buy it, so this is also the shopping list.
_Avoid_: Queue, TBR, Next Up, priority, wishlist, shelf

**Slot**:
One of the five places on the Nightstand. A Slot is freed only by finishing or abandoning the book in it — which is what makes abandoning a deliberate, useful act rather than a failure. A book being read occupies its Slot for the whole time it is being read.
_Avoid_: Position, rank

**Book**:
A work, not an edition. Identity is at the work level so that "do I already own this?" answers correctly regardless of which printing I hold; an edition supplies only cover art and page count.
_Avoid_: Title, volume, copy, edition

**Reason**:
The free-text note, captured when a book is first added, recording who recommended it or what made me want it. It is the primary evidence used when deciding whether a book deserves a Slot, and the defence against a backlog of books I no longer remember wanting.
_Avoid_: Source, note, why, description

**Backlog**:
Every book that is not on the Nightstand. Not a queue and not ordered — an unranked pool that Slots are filled from.
_Avoid_: TBR, library, list

**Studied**:
A self-declared mark that I worked a book seriously rather than merely reading it. Orthogonal to Engagement — a book abandoned halfway can still be studied, and a book read cover to cover need not be.
_Avoid_: Mastered, learned, deep read
